The lookup box above the term field and the English and Dictionary form columns of the word views come from English Wiktionary and its contributors, via Kaikki / Wiktextract (dictionary published 30 October 2026), shared under CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL. Spellings Wiktionary does not cover take the dictionary form the Greek New Testament instance gives the same spelling, from MorphGNT (CC BY-SA 3.0) with Dodson’s public-domain glosses. The site's MIT code licence does not replace those terms. The reduced extract this site uses, and its checksum, are kept in its GitHub repository.

This is a dictionary, not a contextual translation or a scholarly analysis of the text. A dictionary cannot tell which word a spelling is in a given sentence, so each spelling is filed under its likeliest headword, and the lookup reports how many occurrences are in spellings other words share; treat those counts as estimates. The article’s forms always count as the article. About 5–6% of the running text has no dictionary entry, mostly names and Attic forms Wiktionary’s tables omit.

Glosses describe the dictionary word, not the inflected form, and neither glosses nor word-by-word phrase glosses are translations. They are display-only: search terms, counts, and metrics always refer to the Greek words as written.
